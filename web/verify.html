Where each number comes from

The cards
Read from the vault wallet on Solana with the DAS API, not from a list we keep. Grade, certificate and physical vault come from each asset's own on-chain metadata. —
Insured value
The Insured Value attribute written on chain by the custodian. An index they assign — not a market price, and not a number of ours.
The 0.89×
Cards sell for less than the index. 0.89× is the measured median of what the market actually paid (—). Backing is shown after that haircut, not before it.
Market cap
Price decoded from the pool account's sqrt_price × supply from the mint. Both on chain, both read on every load.

What we never do
Add liquidity to backing. Money that came up the curve is not the vault's; it becomes the pool at migration. Two pockets, two numbers, never one total.
